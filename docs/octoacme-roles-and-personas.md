# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA Leads design and execute comprehensive test strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle. They collaborate closely with developers and product managers to identify quality risks and maintain high test coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Validate that features meet quality standards before release
- Identify quality risks and gaps in test coverage
- Automate regression testing and maintain test suites
- Report quality metrics and test results to the team
- Collaborate with developers on design-for-testability principles

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable software
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Build confidence in release readiness
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Planning phase to define test strategy and acceptance criteria
- Daily standups and execution syncs for blocker identification
- Weekly quality reports in team syncs
- Pre-release testing sign-off and verification

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and decision-making authority for projects. They champion initiatives within their organization and ensure alignment with broader business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic rationale for the project
- Make prioritization trade-offs and approve scope changes
- Remove organizational and resource-level blockers
- Give feedback during milestones and product demos
- Champion the project within their organization
- Approve major decisions and release plans

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and strategic alignment
- Remove barriers to team success
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and satisfaction
- Drive organizational support for the project

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status reports
- Attendance at key milestones and product demos
- Ad-hoc escalations and decision requests
- Release announcements and success celebrations

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and risk mitigation strategies. They advise on threat models, review security controls, and coordinate incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements during project planning
- Validate threat mitigation strategies in architecture and design
- Verify security scanning and vulnerability checks in CI/CD
- Approve release processes and deployment procedures
- Advise on risk register items related to security and compliance
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident reviews

### Goals
- Prevent security and compliance breaches
- Ensure projects meet regulatory and organizational standards
- Build secure systems by design
- Enable rapid, secure incident response

### Typical Communication
- Planning phase for security requirements and threat assessment
- Design reviews for security architecture validation
- Release sign-off and pre-deployment verification
- Incident escalations and coordination

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers design and maintain the systems, tools, and processes that enable reliable deployment, monitoring, and operation of software. They ensure efficient CI/CD pipelines and production infrastructure.

### Responsibilities
- Design deployment architecture and infrastructure as code
- Configure and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure provisioning, scaling, and security
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability solutions
- Design and test rollback and disaster recovery procedures
- Coordinate production deployments and handle incidents

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system reliability and uptime
- Reduce deployment risk and incident impact
- Support rapid iteration and scaling

### Typical Communication
- Planning phase for infrastructure requirements and deployment strategy
- Execution standups for deployment blockers and coordination
- Release deployment coordination and verification
- Post-incident reviews and reliability improvements
